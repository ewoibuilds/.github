# Contributing to Ewoi Builds repos

Thank you for helping other learners.

- **Found a mistake or an unclear step?** Open an issue with the repo, the step folder and what
  happened, plus what you expected.
- **Fixing something?** Fork the repo, make a small focused change on a branch, and open a pull
  request that explains what changed and why.
- Keep the step structure: each step folder builds on the one before it.
- Keep secrets out of commits: use `.env.example` with placeholders and keep `.env` on your machine.
- Be kind and patient; everyone here is learning.
